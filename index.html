I am currently an Associate Professor in Computer Science and Software Engineering, Associate Head of Software Engineering Department, College of Computer Science and Software Engineering, Shenzhen University, China. I also serve as a Guest Research Professor at the Institute of Subtropical Agriculture, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from CSE of Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2016, supervised by Prof. Fangzhen Lin. Before that, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from University of Science and Technology of China in 2009. At HKUST, I held a postdoctoral fellowship in Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics and taught as a guest lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering. My research brings knowledge representation and reasoning into deep learning and decision-making, with applications in language, games and healthcare. My group’s project on trustworthy lifelong neuro-symbolic reinforcement learning with explicit knowledge received a 2027 Guangdong Natural Science Foundation General Program grant.

Research directions and collaborations

  1. World models and embodied AI, with physical robots for experiments.
  2. Knowledge representation and reasoning in reinforcement learning, supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, with Fangzhen Lin’s group at HKUST.
  3. Environmental applications, including greenhouse gas emissions forecasting and optimization, and livestock siting, with the Chinese Academy of Sciences and Lin Ma’s group at Nanjing University.
  4. Neural mechanisms of cognition studied through reinforcement learning, with Qi Chen’s group at Shenzhen University’s School of Psychology.
  5. Task-oriented dialogue systems with the State University of New York.
  6. Crowdsourcing for large-scale data with HKUST and the Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
  7. End-to-end models for data mining with WeBank AI.
  8. AI-assisted diagnosis with Guangdong Second Provincial General Hospital.
